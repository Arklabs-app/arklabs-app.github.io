Find your rhythm

01

One thumb. Two taps.

Tap to attach to an anchor. Tap again to release. Catch the next anchor, find PERFECT timing and build Flow.

02

Power-ups along the way

Collect power-ups as you play and learn their effects while improving your overall and per-map records.

03

Your next best score

Compare scores on each map’s world leaderboard through Google Play Games. Online rankings and achievements require an internet connection and a Play Games profile.

Play your way

Learn with the in-game tutorial and choose your music and haptic settings. Maps unlock for free through scores; an optional one-time supporter purchase also unlocks maps. There are no forced advertisements. One optional rewarded ad may offer an extra life per eligible run, subject to availability.
